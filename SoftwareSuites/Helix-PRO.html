Real Bioinformatics

Not a mockup. Real dynamic programming alignments, distance-based phylogenetics, molecular dynamics integrators, and drug docking scores.

Sequence Alignment

Phylogenetics
Neighbor-Joining, UPGMA, maximum likelihood trees. Build phylogenetic trees from distance matrices with real algorithms.
