RESONANCE — Signal Lab
A hardware-style groovebox in one HTML file
How to play
Press Space or ▶ PLAY to start the sequencer.
Click steps in the grid to toggle them. The highlighted column is the current step.
Drag knobs vertically to change parameters. Right-click a knob to reset to default.
Devices
Click any device in the library to add it to the signal chain.
Drag the grip handle ⠿ to reorder. Bypass with B.
Presets
Four built-in tracks: TECHNO · AMBIENT · LOFI · BREAKS.
Export
Press ↓ to render and download a WAV of the current pattern.
