About

  

    Nexus Atlas v1.0

    Single-file multi-tool productivity suite. 100% offline capable. All data stays on your device in localStorage.


    Modules: Dashboard, Tasks, Notes, Habits, Focus, Finance, Workout, Reading, Calculator, Unit Converter, Text Tools, Dev Tools, Color Tools, Live Data, Analytics, Settings.


    Zero external dependencies. All charts drawn on Canvas 2D. All APIs are free and keyless.
