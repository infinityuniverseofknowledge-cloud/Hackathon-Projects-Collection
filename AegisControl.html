  • Hohmann transfers, bi-elliptic, plane changes, ΔV budgets
  • Tsiolkovsky rocket equation, Isp calculations, staging
  • Link budget with Friis equation, Doppler, free-space path loss

  • Ground station network simulation (DSN sites)
  • Launch sequence simulation with T-minus events
  • Spacecraft subsystem health monitoring
